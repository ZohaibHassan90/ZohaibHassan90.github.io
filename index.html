Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about pricing, timelines, process, and how we start working together.

Most work is scoped as a fixed milestone price for a clear feature set, or hourly/weekly for ongoing product work. After a short discovery call I send a written estimate with scope, assumptions, and payment milestones — no surprise invoices mid-build. Smaller MVPs and feature add-ons can start lean; larger apps are broken into phases so you only fund what you need next.

A focused MVP usually lands in about 4–8 weeks. Mid-size products with auth, payments, and several core flows often take 2–4 months. Complex multi-module apps can run longer and are planned in phases. Timeline depends on designs readiness, API availability, and review speed — I share a realistic schedule before kickoff, not after.

1) Brief + goals — what must ship and what can wait. 2) Scope & estimate — milestones, tech approach, and delivery plan. 3) Build in short cycles with regular demos (TestFlight / internal builds). 4) Polish, QA, and store submission support. 5) Handoff with clean code, setup notes, and optional post-launch support. You always know what’s done, what’s next, and what’s blocked.

You do. On final payment (or per agreed milestone), you get full ownership of the project source, assets created for the work, and repository access. I can develop in your GitHub/GitLab from day one so nothing is locked on my side. Third-party licenses (fonts, paid SDKs, store accounts) stay under your accounts.

Yes. Every build includes a short handoff window for fixes tied to the delivered scope. After that, we can set a retainer or on-demand hours for updates, store compliance, crash fixes, and new features. I also document how to run, build, and release the app so your team isn’t stuck.
